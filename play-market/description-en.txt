Four Adits is a fast first-person shooter where the ground can be blown up, dug through and collapsed. Up to four teams land on one map with a cave-filled mountain in the center, ringed by trenches. Matches last only a few minutes before the zone starts closing in.

MINE, BUY, FIGHT
You start with nothing but a pick. Mine coal, iron, gold and crystals: the closer to the center, the more valuable the ore. Sell it at your base shop and buy a pistol, assault rifle, sniper rifle, shotgun, RPG-7, grenades, mines, body armor and medkits.

DESTRUCTIBLE WORLD
Rockets and grenades leave craters, mining charges blast out whole caverns, and the drill cuts new tunnels. Build cover from sandbag walls or collapse the ground under your enemies.

GEAR AND DEFENSE
• FPV drones in three levels, flown in first person
• A ballistic missile aimed on the map
• Turrets: the defense turret fires whatever weapon you load into it, the medic turret heals allies, the launcher turret throws grenades

6 MODES
• Last team standing: limited lives and a closing zone
• Free for all
• Capture the flag
• Zombies: everyone together against the waves
• King of the hill
• Class war: 18 classes with their own gear and abilities

5 MAPS
Plains, desert, a city with high-rise buildings, jungle and tall mountains.

RANDOM EVENTS
Grenade rain, night, low gravity, double damage, minefields, teleports and twenty more events that change the course of a match. You can turn them off in the lobby.

PLAY WITH FRIENDS
Create a room and send a friend the code, no sign-up needed. You can even play one on one. Voice chat for everyone or just your team, plus text chat with a profanity filter. You can block and report players. Practice against bots offline. Once you are out of lives, fly freely around the map and watch the fight.

PROGRESS AND REWARDS
Levels, daily quests, a free 30-tier battle pass and achievements. Matches earn in-game coins to spend on hats, outfits, back items, pets, cases and new classes. Coins are earned only by playing; there are no real-money purchases.

CONTROLS
Move and resize the touch buttons. Gamepad, keyboard and mouse are supported. Graphics and sensitivity settings included. Available in English and Russian.

An internet connection is required to play with friends.
